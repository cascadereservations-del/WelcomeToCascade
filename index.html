Boracay of the South White Sand

Casual Eateries & Local Legends

Tiongson Arcade

Lagao District

Local Favorite Budget-Friendly 6:00 PM onwards · Open Late

GenSan's most beloved evening ritual — an open-air street food arcade where the night is built around freshly caught seafood cooked to order. Point at your choice from the display, and minutes later it arrives sizzling. The essentials: grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw (shellfish in broth), sizzling bihod (tuna roe), and calamares. Arrive after six, settle in, and let the smoky aroma do the rest.

🚕 Grab · approx. 15 min from property

Must-Try

Grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw, bihod

Best Time

Evening onwards for lively atmosphere

📍 Navigate to Arcade

Chicken Hauz

Local Institution

A GenSan institution that locals measure all other chicken against. The house specialty — garlic buttered chicken, slow-cooked until the meat pulls effortlessly from the bone, deeply savory and unmistakably aromatic — has drawn devotees across generations. Pair it with kinilaw or pancit canton and a cold mango shake for the full experience. Simple surroundings, honest prices, and a flavor that stays with you.

📍 Find Location →
🍗

Tambilawan

Halal · Filipino · Inasal

GenSan's first and only Halal-certified restaurant, and a genuine institution. Dine kamayan-style — meals served on banana leaves, eaten with your hands — in open-air traditional nipa huts. The chicken sisig is a city-wide favorite: crunchy, savory, and deeply satisfying. Order the nilagpang (grilled chicken slow-simmered in broth), and finish with their celebrated buko halo-halo, finely shaved and generously layered. Halal throughout.

🧺 Washing Machine

1

Load clothes into the drum — do not exceed ¾ full for best results.

2

Add detergent to the detergent tray on top (main compartment, not pre-wash).

3

Turn the program dial to your preferred wash cycle (e.g. Normal for everyday clothes).

4

Press the Start/Pause button. A typical wash takes about 45 minutes.

💡 Laundry detergent is provided. Leave the lid open after use to keep the drum fresh.

🍳 Induction Stove

1

Place a flat-bottomed pot or pan on the centre of the cooking surface.

2

Press the Power button to turn on the unit.

3

Use the + / − buttons to adjust the heat level. Start at a medium setting.

4

Press Power again to turn off when done. The surface will be cool to the touch quickly.

💡 Induction only works with magnetic cookware. All pots in the unit are compatible. Wipe the glass surface clean after use.

🚿 Water Heater

✓

The water heater is already switched on by our team before your arrival — no switches to find.

1

Turn on the shower and wait 1–2 minutes for the hot water to flow through.

2

Adjust the temperature by turning the shower knob — towards hot for warmer water, towards cold to cool it down.

💡 Water takes a moment to warm up — this is normal. If it stays cold after 2 minutes, message Marifel and we'll check it immediately.

👔 Cloth Steamer

1

Remove the water tank, fill with clean water, and reattach it to the unit.

2

Plug in and switch on. Wait about 30–45 seconds until you see steady steam.

3

Hold the nozzle 1–2 cm from the fabric and move it slowly downward.

4

Hang the garment while steaming for best results. Unplug and empty any remaining water after use.

💡 Safe for most fabrics including linen, cotton, and light synthetics. Test on an inconspicuous area first for delicate items.
